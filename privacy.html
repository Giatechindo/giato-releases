Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025 · Built with local-first privacy principles.

1. Local-First Architecture

Giato operates as a desktop application designed to run directly on your personal computer or workstation. We believe your code, project files, and development workflows belong strictly to you. Giato does not transmit, index, or store your private codebase on any external server managed by us.

2. Data Handling & Storage

All project settings, agent session logs, execution history, and preferences are stored locally on your device within your standard user directories. No proprietary source code is uploaded to cloud storage or mirrored off your machine.

3. Third-Party AI Providers & API Keys

When you use cloud AI capabilities (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or other LLM providers), prompts and requested context snippets are sent directly from your device over encrypted HTTPS connections using your own provided API keys. We do not proxy, inspect, or retain your API credentials or communication with third-party providers. You may also run Giato entirely offline using local LLM engines (such as Ollama).

4. Telemetry & Analytics

Giato does not track keystrokes, clipboard contents, file system paths, or personal user data. Only minimal, anonymous application crash reports or update checks may be requested to ensure software reliability, with no identifying details included.

5. Your Rights & Control

Because all data resides locally on your machine, you have complete control over your files at all times. You can inspect, modify, export, or delete any cached session or setting directly from your computer without needing to contact any service provider.

6. Contact & Open Source

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy or Giato's security practices, please visit our official GitHub repository at github.com/Giatechindo.
